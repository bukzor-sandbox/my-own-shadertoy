# Mission

bukzor comes to understand WebGPU and GPGPU programming, in practice, by
building and owning a compute-first Shadertoy-alike. Understanding, not
memorization: what each part is for and how the parts fit, well enough to build
with them; names and signatures are looked up. The toy is the vehicle; bukzor's
understanding is the product, and bukzor is the one who benefits.

Starting point: concepts known vaguely, practice none -- one triangle, long
forgotten. TypeScript is a means to an end and is expected to shrink to WebGPU
glue and interpreters once the authoring side moves to Pyodide, Rust wasm, or
Lean wasm.
