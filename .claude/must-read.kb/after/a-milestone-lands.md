# After a milestone lands

A milestone's claim in `milestone-ladder.kb/` has just dropped `todo: true`.

1. List generously, from its diff, every concept the milestone first uses that
   is relevant to the project (WebGPU APIs and WGSL features, commonly, but not
   only) into its `first-uses:`, each with `evidence:`. Ask bukzor to strike
   what wasn't learning and confirm the rest. A first use Claude typed is a
   violation: leave it off `first-uses:`, fix it, and handle it as [overreach]
   says. Ledger: [FIRST_INSTANCE].
2. Point bukzor at the webgpufundamentals.org articles on that milestone's
   topics, to compare with what bukzor wrote. Ledger: [TUTORIAL_READING].
